Primary analysis: OpenMx

These five reports contain the revised Gaussian ML/REML analyses, including all 11 candidate specifications in manuscript Table 1. The Family 2 specification f2_complete_drift was selected for all seven outcome sets. Selection uses ML BIC; reliability and projections use the corresponding REML fit.

ReportOutcome sets

The multivariate models jointly estimate the trait covariance matrix for every retained source and the residual. Equal-weight and SNR composites use the full covariance matrices. The three Mental-Health groups are stress; anxiety or depression; and bipolar disorder, personality disorder or suicidal ideation. Group indicators can overlap.

All seven outcome sets reached 1,000 accepted bootstrap fits and 100 completed jackknife fits. Attempt counts, finite-estimate counts, unavailable intervals and sparse-tail flags remain in the reports. The final HTML refresh reused those completed fits; no fitting was performed for this publication package.

The two multivariate reports include selected-model variance percentages calculated from saved REML components, with full-precision CSV downloads. This reporting refresh adds no model fitting or resampling.
