Provenance issues preserved with the outputs

Mental Health: the retained notebook reads original_label as human_label; that field holds the gpt-4o-mini screening label carried from u_label, not the source-corpus label. The reference-label audit recovers the source-corpus status for all 100 items and reproduces the Mental-Health agreement values in Supplementary Tables S2 and S15.

Hate Speech: saved outputs contain 98 retained reference items and 7,056 paired comparisons, while some historical labels say 100 and 7,200. The discrepancy is disclosed rather than recomputed.
