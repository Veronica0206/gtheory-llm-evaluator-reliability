Supportive analysis: lme4 and method of moments

These are the earlier R1 lme4 reports and the retained method-of-moments (MoM) diagnostics. Their code and saved numerical outputs are preserved. The OpenMx reports supply the revised primary estimates.

  • Hate-Speech: ordinal outcome
  • Mental-Health: seven- and three-level outcomes
  • Drug-Review: holistic sentiment
  • Mental-Health: multivariate outcomes
  • Drug-Review: four aspects

The multivariate lme4 reports fit traits separately and use diagonal composites. Mental-Health’s three-condition subset contains depression, anxiety and suicidal ideation; it is different from OpenMx’s three OR-group model. These reports are not a joint lme4 residual-structure sensitivity and should not be read as a comparison differing only by software.

Retained supplementary analyses

Method-of-moments index: signed saturated components, residual composition, scoring/rank sensitivities and entropy-stratum reliability. Other-analysis index: facet severity and descriptive cross-trait/binary-scale diagnostics.
