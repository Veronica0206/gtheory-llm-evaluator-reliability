Supportive analysis: lme4 and method of moments

These five lme4 reports contain the completed 10 September 2026 point-estimate analyses. All eleven family specifications are compared by ML, and the lowest-BIC member within Family 2 is refitted by REML for components, reliability and D-study projections. All seven outcome sets selected f2_complete_drift. The OpenMx reports remain the primary manuscript analysis.

  • Hate-Speech: ordinal outcome
  • Mental-Health: seven- and three-level outcomes
  • Drug-Review: holistic sentiment
  • Mental-Health: six flags and three OR groups
  • Drug-Review: four aspects

The multivariate reports fit traits together in a stacked model with trait-specific diagonal source variances and one residual variance shared across traits. Cross-trait covariances are fixed at zero. These are the covariance restrictions of the original submission, applied to the current eleven specifications; comparison with full-covariance OpenMx estimates is a covariance-restriction sensitivity.

Mental-Health includes a six-flag model and a separate three-OR-group model: stress; anxiety or depression; and bipolar disorder, personality disorder or suicidal ideation. The group indicators may overlap. Drug-Review includes four aspects: efficacy, safety, burden and cost.

The reports contain point estimates only, without bootstrap, jackknife or confidence intervals. Each HTML includes the complete downloadable Rmd source, fit diagnostics and saved output downloads—106 files across the five reports. Data and reproduction instructions describe fresh runs and reuse of matching local point-fit checkpoints.

Univariate supplementary diagnostics

Method-of-moments index: signed saturated components, component absorption, scoring/rank sensitivities and entropy-stratum reliability. Other-analysis index: retained agreement and calibration analyses, with links to descriptive facet-severity point estimates.

Archived interval and cross-trait evidence

The manuscript’s historical facet-severity intervals are preserved separately: Hate-Speech, Mental-Health, and Drug-Review. Retained Mental-Health and Drug-Review cross-trait diagnostics also have focused reports. These contain earlier saved code and outputs, with source-specific provenance; they are not part of the new point-only lme4 model fits above.
